What is firewood sales?

Seasoned firewood is wood that has been split and left to dry until most of its moisture is gone. Dry hardwood catches fire easily, burns hotter, and makes less smoke and creosote than green wood.

When do you need firewood sales?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • You heat with a wood stove or fireplace.
  • You want firewood for a fire pit or outdoor kitchen.
  • You'd rather buy wood cut from local trees than shipped-in bundles.
