## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Balanced across four audiences (user's decision), each with its own door:
1. **Schools & districts** — K-5 (new), high school CTE pathways, colleges, workforce partners. Buying a curriculum/program for students.
2. **Individual pilots** — adults (and 16+) getting FAA Part 107 certified and building a drone career.
3. **Businesses** — need aerial work done (construction, thermal, real estate, photography) via AINautics services or the iFlyDrone nationwide pilot network.
4. **Entrepreneurs** — want to start their own drone school through the Ambassador Program.

## Product Purpose

AINautics trains the people who fly drones — and puts them to work. Training (AINautics University), services, and a nationwide pilot network (iFlyDrone) under one brand. Success for the concept site: the owner sees one unified, premium brand that wins the contract.

## Positioning

A full pipeline, not just a course: classroom → FAA certificate → paid flights through their own pilot network. Rooted in Columbia, SC since 2013; founder-led (Chris Williams); minority-owned; mission to close the diversity and talent gaps in the drone industry (South Carolina Drone Initiative).

## Operating Context

- In-person training at the Columbia, SC facility; self-paced online courses; a VR / drone simulation mobile lab that travels to schools and events.
- Program partners (from public press): Goodwill Industries of Upstate/Midlands SC (ELEVATE, free program, ages 16+), Benedict College B.E.S.T. Lives Center, Orangeburg-Calhoun Technical College, Florence 1 Schools, Jasper County School District (Ridgeland-Hardeeville HS drone pathway).
- Serves GA, SC, NC for training; iFlyDrone network is nationwide.
