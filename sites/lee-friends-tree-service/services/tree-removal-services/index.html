What is tree removal?

Tree removal means cutting a tree down in controlled sections, lowering the pieces safely, and hauling the wood away. A tree with open space around it can sometimes be felled whole; a tree beside a house, fence, or driveway is taken down in pieces so nothing falls where it shouldn't.

What's included

  • Hazardous tree removal
  • Dead tree removal
  • Clearing space for projects
  • Tree debris removal and hauling
  • Full property tree removal

When do you need tree removal?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • The tree is dead or rotting, with bare limbs or peeling bark.
  • It leans toward your house, driveway, or a neighbor's property.
  • The trunk is cracked, split, or hollow.
  • A storm has damaged it beyond saving.
  • It hangs over your roof or crowds a structure.
  • You need room for an addition, pool, or backyard upgrade.

How does tree removal work with Lee Friend's?

Here's what happens from your first call to the finished job.

  1. Call for a free estimate

    Call (770) 466-5112 or send the quote form with a few details about the tree.

  2. We assess the tree

  4. Clean-up and recycling

Can you remove a tree that's right next to my house?

Yes. Trees close to houses, fences, and garages are taken down in small sections, so pieces never drop onto the roof. One customer's 100-year-old pine stood between a wood fence, a driveway, and an attached garage.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

Some cities in the Atlanta area require a permit for certain trees, and many homeowners don't need one. We know the permit requirements in the areas we serve, so ask us when we come out to look at the tree.
