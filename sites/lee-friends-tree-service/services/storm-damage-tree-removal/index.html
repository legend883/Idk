What is storm damage tree removal?

Storm damage tree removal is the clean-up after wind, ice, or lightning brings down a tree or big limbs. It covers trees on houses, cars, fences, and driveways, plus split or hanging limbs that could still fall.

When do you need storm damage tree removal?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • A tree or large limb has fallen on your house, garage, or car.
  • A downed tree blocks your driveway or street access.
  • Limbs are cracked and hanging in the canopy after a storm.
  • A tree is newly leaning, or the soil around its roots has lifted.
  • You need photos and documentation for an insurance claim.

How does storm damage tree removal work with Lee Friend's?

Here's what happens from your first call to the finished job.

  1. Get safe, then call

    Keep everyone away from the tree and any wires. If power lines are involved, call Georgia Power first. Then call us at (770) 466-5112.

  2. We assess the damage

    We look at how the tree is resting and what it's pressing on, then plan a safe way to lift it off.

  3. Removal and clean-up

    The crew cuts and lifts the tree away in sections and clears fallen limbs and debris from the property.

  4. Insurance coordination

    We give you the details your insurance company asks for and help coordinate with them.

Questions about storm damage tree removal

Does homeowners insurance cover a fallen tree?

Usually yes, when a tree falls on a covered structure such as your house, garage, or fence because of a storm. Policies often don't pay to remove a tree that fell without hitting anything, or one that fell because it was already dead. Check your own policy, and we'll help coordinate with your insurance company.

What should I do first when a tree falls on my house?

Get everyone out of the damaged part of the house and stay away from the tree and any downed wires. Call Georgia Power if lines are involved, take photos for your insurance company, then call us.
