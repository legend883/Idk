## Platform

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS (delegated: no build step, so the site can be handed to the client and hosted anywhere).

## Users
North Atlanta homeowners (Sandy Springs, Buckhead, Roswell, Alpharetta, Milton, Dunwoody) planning a significant backyard project: an outdoor kitchen, outdoor fireplace, stone patio, or pool deck. They have budget ($15K+ jobs), are comparing two or three contractors, and want to know: is this crew skilled, trustworthy, and worth the premium?

Secondary audience (for the pitch): the owner, Scott Arnold, who will judge whether this site reflects 40 years of craft.

## Product Purpose
Website redesign pitch for Arnold Masonry & Landscape. The site's job: make a high-end homeowner believe this is a true master-mason firm, and get them to request a design consultation. Built by Legend Creative as a free demo to win the client.

## Positioning
Master masons, not a landscaping crew that also lays pavers. Scott Arnold learned the trade at 17 under an older master mason after moving from Long Island to Atlanta, and founded the company in 1985. Brick and stone are the core; outdoor kitchens, fireplaces, chimneys, and patios are built as permanent masonry structures.

## Operating Context
Mobile-first browsing (homeowners scroll on phones in the evening, then call). Contractors are compared on Google reviews, Houzz, and project photos. The consultation call is the conversion.
