# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Scope: this repo builds sites for many Legend Creative clients. This record covers the dock-builder client site in `sites/calder-dock/`.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static site (HTML/CSS/JS, built with Vite + React + Tailwind so Magic UI components can be used as real code; output is a plain static folder that can be hosted anywhere). Chosen because the repo's workflow requires Magic UI components and the client needs no CMS yet.

## Users

- **Waterfront homeowners (primary):** own lakefront property, planning a new dock, boat lift, or replacing a tired one. Usually researching on a phone in the evening, comparing two or three builders, worried about permits, timelines, and whether it will survive storms and water-level swings.
- **Marinas, HOAs, and commercial waterfront (secondary):** property managers and owners who need multi-slip docks, repairs, and a contractor who can handle permitting and heavier marine construction.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for a dock-building contractor. Success = a qualified visitor requests a site visit / quote (form or phone call).

## Positioning

Premium craftsman: they design and build docks to be engineered, permitted, and built for decades, not seasons. Confirmed by user: "premium craftsman" feeling, both residential and commercial clients.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate builders by past work photos, materials offered, process clarity (site visit → design → permits → build), and responsiveness. Quote requests usually need property address, water body, and project type.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services assumed typical for the trade (INFERRED, confirm with client): new fixed/piling docks, floating docks, boat lifts & boathouses, repairs & re-decking, seawalls / shoreline, commercial & marina docks.
- Business name, location, phone, email, license info: **INFERRED placeholders** — "Calder Dock & Marine", Lake Norman, NC. Must be replaced with real details.

## Brand Commitments

None yet (no logo, colors, or fonts). Feeling requested: premium craftsman.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real project photos, testimonials, reviews, years-in-business, or certifications were provided. Do not fabricate testimonials or reviews.
- Photos on the site are AI-generated stand-ins (user approved) and must be swapped for real job photos.
- Any numbers shown (counts, years) are placeholders the client must confirm.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: craftsmanship is proven by photos and construction detail, not adjectives.
2. Make the process feel handled: permits, engineering, and timeline explained plainly.
3. One clear next step everywhere: request a site visit.
4. Speak to both homeowners and commercial clients without diluting the homeowner experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline; readable on phones outdoors (strong contrast).
