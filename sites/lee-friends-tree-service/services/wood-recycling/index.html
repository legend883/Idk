Eco-Friendly Wood Recycling in Loganville, GA

Lee Friend's Tree Service recycles the wood from its jobs in Loganville, GA instead of sending it to a landfill. Branches become mulch, good hardwood and pine logs go to local mills for lumber, leftover hardwood becomes firewood, and wood chips are free.

What is eco-friendly wood recycling?

Wood recycling means every part of a tree we cut gets a second use. Small branches go through a chipper and become mulch. Larger hardwood and pine logs are taken to local sawmills. Hardwood that isn't milled is split and seasoned into firewood.

When do you need eco-friendly wood recycling?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • You'd like your tree job to send as little as possible to the landfill.
  • You want free wood chips for garden beds, paths, or play areas.
  • You'd like seasoned firewood cut from local trees.

How does eco-friendly wood recycling work with Lee Friend's?

Here's what happens from your first call to the finished job.

  1. Chipping

    Branches and small limbs go through the chipper on site and become wood chips.

  2. Sorting the logs

    Straight hardwood and pine logs are set aside for local mills.

  3. To the mill

    Those logs are delivered to local mills and sawn into lumber.

  4. Firewood

    Leftover hardwood is split and seasoned for firewood sales.
