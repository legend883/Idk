## Who they are
- AINautics, Columbia, South Carolina. Founded 2013 by Chris (Christopher) Williams.
- "A premier provider of state-of-the-art drone training and custom drone services in the Georgia, South and North Carolina area."
- Mission: "empower individuals and communities through high-quality drone education and certification programs."
- "Over 5,000 pilots trained, including teens, veterans, and working professionals."
- "National leader in drone workforce development."
- Columbia Chamber of Commerce **Small Business of the Year 2024**. Minority-owned (Midlands Minority Business Accelerator spotlight).
- Homepage line: "We empower aspiring and seasoned drone pilots through expert-led training programs, a robust pilot community, and access to exclusive resources."
- Instagram line: "Most drone courses stop at theory. AINautics trains…" / site: "graduates don't just pass the test, they're ready for real-world work."

## Three brands
1. **ainautics.com**: training, services, schools, Ambassador program, pilot network, job board, blog, events.
2. **AINautics University** (ainauticsuniversity.com): "FAA-certified training in drones, AI, and robotics, building the skilled workforce behind tomorrow's autonomous systems." Areas: FAA Certification, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Autonomous Systems, Workforce Development.
3. **iFlyDrone** (iflydrone.com): "Connecting Clients with Professional Drone Pilots Nationwide."
   (Also: **AINautics Mobile Lab**, a VR bus/trailer with drone simulators for schools and events.)

## Training
- FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certification: in person, online, and SELF-PACED. Ages 16+.
- Commercial Drone Certification Bootcamp (intensive, hands-on).
- "Fast-Track Your Drone Career": Part 107 exam prep, specialized industry training, career pathway guidance.
- Drone Delivery RPIC Course: five days in person in Columbia, "from ground school to flying a fixed-wing delivery drone yourself". Requires Part 107. Leads to roles such as Drone Delivery Pilot, Fleet Operations Supervisor, and Delivery Mission Coordinator.
- AG Remote Pilot Certification and Farming Commercial Drone Pilot Certification. Rural agriculture program run with a leading land-grant university.
- Public safety / law enforcement tactical program: covers Part 107 through tactical deployment. Two-day in-person cohorts for officers, School Resource Officers, EMTs and firefighters.
- Specialized training for teams in real estate, construction, agriculture, public safety and emergency management.
- Adult Academy. Drone Programming Basics (hands-on curriculum by Christopher Williams).

## Schools
- High school: STEM/CTE pathways, from Part 107 prep through drone engineering and operations, with turnkey curriculum.
- UAS Student Scholars Remote Pilot Certification (K-12).
- Colleges and universities: from single-day intensives to semester-long courses, for credit or not.
- Homeschool: SC Homeschool Expo, with families integrating drones into homeschool curricula.
- **NEW (not on their sites yet): K-5 elementary drone programs.**

## Services
- Construction progress, thermal inspection ("find trouble spots on roofs without a ladder"), infrastructure inspection, aerial photography and video, real estate, weddings, promotional video, surveying and mapping.
- iFlyDrone: aerial imagery, mapping (3D models, topo maps, volumetrics), inspection (infrastructure, power lines), agriculture (NDVI/multispectral), thermal (energy audits, solar).
- iFlyDrone in 5 steps: specify needs → matched with vetted certified pilots → review & schedule → receive deliverables → secure payment (escrow until approved).
- Pilots: create profile, upload certifications, get approved; every pilot is Part 107 certified and vetted.

## Business programs
- Ambassador Program, "Start Your Own Drone School": curriculum, business support (marketing templates, pricing guides), mentorship and networking. No drone experience required.
- Pilot network + job board, and career development (job placement assistance).
