3Business

Aerial work, flown by pilots we trained.

Aerial photography, inspections and survey data across Georgia and the Carolinas, and anywhere in the U.S. through iFlyDrone. Every pilot is FAA certified.

Construction

Regular progress photos, clearer views of site issues for subcontractors, and better safety awareness.

Thermal

Heat maps of roofs, buildings and solar arrays for energy audits and leak detection.

Mapping and survey

3D models, topographic maps and volume measurements for land development and mining.

Inspection

Infrastructure, power lines and facilities, inspected with precision while crews stay safely on the ground.

Agriculture

NDVI and multispectral maps that show crop stress, soil conditions and irrigation patterns.

Photo and video

Aerial imagery for real estate listings, weddings, events and promotional films.
