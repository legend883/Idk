# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a tiny vanilla JS file, generated by `build.py` from one data file so the business name, phone, hours, and schema stay identical on every page. No framework, no build server; any static host works.

## Users

Homeowners and property managers in Loganville, Walton County, Gwinnett County, and Metro Atlanta who have a tree problem (a dead or leaning tree, a storm-downed tree, a stump, overgrown limbs) or want seasoned firewood. Many are calling from a phone after a storm. Secondary readers: AI search tools (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) checking who the company is, what it does, where, and what credentials back it.

## Product Purpose

Private preview of a new website for Lee Friend's Tree Service, Inc. Success: a visitor knows within one screen who they are, what they do, where, and why to trust them, and calls (770) 466-5112 or sends a quote request.

## Positioning

A family-owned, husband-and-wife tree company in Loganville with an ISA Certified Arborist on staff, recycling the wood it cuts into mulch, lumber, and firewood.

## Brand Commitments

Name exactly "Lee Friend's Tree Service, Inc." Phone (770) 466-5112. Loganville, GA 30052. No existing visual brand was available to carry over.

## Evidence on Hand

Facts from the owner brief and the current site (leefriendstreeservice.com, read through search results because the domain was blocked from the build environment). No photos could be retrieved yet; photo slots carry [CONFIRM] labels. Never invent facts, reviews, numbers, awards, or credentials; anything unverified carries a visible [CONFIRM: ...] tag.

## Product Principles

1. Answer first: every heading is a question a customer asks, answered in one or two plain sentences.
2. One set of business facts, everywhere, matching the schema exactly.
3. Calling is always one tap away.
4. Proof over adjectives: credentials, the arborist by name, real reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, large tap targets, readable on a phone in daylight, reduced motion respected.
