What is firewood sales?

Seasoned firewood is wood that has been split and left to dry until most of its moisture is gone. Dry hardwood catches fire easily, burns longer and steadier, and makes less smoke than green wood. Ours is cut to practical sizes that fit most fireplaces, stoves, and fire pits.

What's included

  • Seasoned hardwood for steady burning
  • Clean cuts sized for easy stacking
  • Low moisture for easy lighting
  • Supply sourced from our tree work
  • Delivery or pickup

When do you need firewood sales?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • You heat with a fireplace or wood stove.
  • You want wood for a fire pit or outdoor cooking.
  • You run a restaurant with wood-fired equipment.
  • You'd rather buy local wood than shipped-in bundles.
