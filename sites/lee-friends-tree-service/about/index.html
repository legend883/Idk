About Lee Friend's Tree Service

Lee Friend's Tree Service, Inc. is a family-owned tree company in Loganville, GA, founded in 1989 by a husband-and-wife team and still run by them today, with ISA Certified Arborist Jennifer Perrone on staff. [CONFIRM: Founding year: the current site says 1989 everywhere, but some outside listings say 1991. Confirm 1989]

How did Lee Friend's Tree Service start?

The company began in 1989 as a passion project for a husband-and-wife team who loved working outdoors and wanted to build something meaningful.

More than 30 years later, it has grown into a respected name in tree care, still offering direct, personal service from a local team that works for homeowners and businesses alike. Loganville is home, and the company has built its reputation here on reliable results and honest advice. What makes the company different?

A certified arborist on staff, wood recycling on every job, and a mascot dog that adds personality to the crew.

Every job, big or small, gets the same attention to detail. Branches become mulch, hardwood and pine logs go to local mills, and leftover hardwood is split and seasoned into firewood. [CONFIRM: The mascot dog's name and a photo]
