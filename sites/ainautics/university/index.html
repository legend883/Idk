About

Drone Air Innovations, built in Columbia.

At AINautics, we empower aspiring and seasoned drone pilots through expert-led training programs, a robust pilot community and access to exclusive resources.

Chris Williams founded AINautics in 2013 to prepare students and professionals for the unmanned aircraft industry. Today the minority-owned company runs a family of brands that together form a complete drone workforce pipeline.
