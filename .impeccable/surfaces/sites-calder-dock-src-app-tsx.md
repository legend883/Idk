## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a nautical chart of the client's water. Every claim is plotted: depths, piles, coves, jobs. Refuses the category default of a sunset-dock hero with a serif headline over a dark overlay.

OWN-WORLD: Chart paper white ground, shallow-water chart blue owning whole bands, buff land tint only inside drawn charts, chart magenta for every action, note and pin, navy chart ink, thin contour linework, italic serif for water names and soundings (Spectral italic), wide grotesk headlines (Archivo, expanded). STORY: Visitor sees a builder that measures before it builds; sees their own kind of dock plotted on the lake; understands water-level swings are engineered for; requests a site sounding visit.
