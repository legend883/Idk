Eco-Friendly Wood Recycling in Loganville, GA

Lee Friend's Tree Service recycles the wood from its jobs in Loganville, GA instead of sending it to a landfill. Branches become mulch, hardwood and pine logs go to local mills, and leftover hardwood is split and seasoned into firewood.

What is eco-friendly wood recycling?

Wood recycling means every part of a tree we cut gets a second use. Small debris goes through our chipper and becomes wood chips for mulch and landscaping. Larger hardwood and pine logs are delivered to local mills, where they're turned into paper, furniture, or milled wood. Leftover hardwood is cut and split into firewood.

What's included

  • Garden mulch sharing
  • Firewood splitting
  • Log transport to local mills
  • Hardwood repurposing
  • Recycled mulch for landscaping

When do you need eco-friendly wood recycling?

Call when a tree shows any of these signs, or if you're unsure and want an arborist's opinion.

  • You'd like your tree job to send nothing to the landfill.
  • You want wood chips for garden beds, paths, or play areas.
  • You'd like seasoned firewood cut from local trees.

How does eco-friendly wood recycling work with Lee Friend's?

Here's what happens from your first call to the finished job.

  1. Chipping

    Smaller debris goes through our wood chipper and becomes chips for mulching and landscaping.

  2. Sorting the logs

    Larger hardwood and pine pieces are set aside for the mill.

  3. To the mill

    Those logs are delivered to local mills and turned into paper, furniture, or milled wood products.

  4. Firewood

    Leftover hardwood is cut, split, and seasoned for firewood.
